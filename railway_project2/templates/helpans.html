{% if question == "Can I cancel my Train ticket?" %}
How to Cancel an E-Ticket

💠 Log in to your account on the IRCTC Official Website or app.

💠 Go to the "Booked Tickets" link under your account menu.

💠 Select the ticket you want to cancel.

💠 Choose the specific passengers if you need a partial cancellation.

💠 Confirm the cancellation to process your refund.

Key Rules to Remember
Chart Preparation:

You cannot cancel e-tickets online after the reservation chart has been prepared. You must file a TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt) online instead.

Refund Timeline:

Refunds are typically credited back to the original payment account within 5 to 7 working days.

Counter Tickets:

Tickets bought physically at a railway counter cannot be canceled through a regular user login on the main site; they require the Counter Ticket Cancellation Portal or a PRS counter

{% elif question == "What is the baggage allowance?" %}
AC First Class:

70 kg free (maximum up to 150 kg with paid excess charges).

AC 2-Tier & First Class:

50 kg free (maximum up to 100 kg with paid excess charges).

Sleeper Class:

40 kg free (maximum up to 80 kg with paid excess charges).

Second Class (General):

35 kg free (maximum up to 70 kg with paid excess charges).

AC 3-Tier, AC 3 Economy & AC Chair Car:

40 kg limit (free allowance and maximum limit are both 40 kg. paid excess luggage is not permitted in these classes).

{% elif question == "Can I get a refund?" %}
Confirmed Regular Tickets:

Cancel up to 4 hours before the train leaves to get a partial refund minus cancellation fees.

Confirmed Tatkal Tickets:

You do not get a refund if you cancel a confirmed Tatkal ticket.

RAC and Waitlisted Tickets:

Cancel up to 30 minutes before departure to get a refund minus a small flat clerkage fee.

Fully Cancelled Trains:

If Indian Railways cancels the train, you get a full automatic refund with no need to cancel manually.

Delayed Trains:

If your train is delayed by more than 3 hours and you choose not to travel, you can file a TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt) for a full refund

{% elif question == "Is food available on trains?" %}
Onboard Pantry Cars:

Many Mail, Express, Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Duronto trains have pantry cars that serve cooked meals, snacks, and beverages directly at your seat.

IRCTC eCatering ("Food on Track"):

You can order meals from authorized local restaurants and food chains (like Haldiram's or Domino's) to be delivered right to your seat at hundreds of major railway stations

Partner Apps:

You can order food using the official IRCTC eCatering Website, the "Food on Track" app, or partner delivery services like Zomato and Swiggy by entering your 10-digit PNR number

Pre-Booking:

Premium trains include meals in the ticket fare, and select Express trains now offer an E-Pantry option to pre-order meals during ticket booking

Phone Orders:

You can call the toll-free number 1323 to place a food order over the phone

{% elif question == "Do Children need ticket?" %}
Children Under 5 Years Old
No ticket / Fare needed:

If the child travels without a separate seat or berth and shares one with a parent, they travel completely free. You must still enter their name and age in the passenger details during booking, though their name will not appear on the final reservation chart.

Full fare for a berth:

If you want a dedicated seat or berth for your child under 5, you must pay the full adult fare.

Children Aged 5 to Under 12 Years Old
Half fare (No Berth):

If you choose the "No Seat/No Berth" (NOSB) option during booking, you pay half of the adult fare, and the child shares your seat/berth.

Full fare (With Berth):

If you want a separate seat or berth assigned to the child, you must pay the full adult fare.

Children 12 Years and Older
Full adult ticket:

Anyone aged 12 or older is considered an adult by Indian Railways and must purchase a full-fare ticket with a designated seat or berth.
