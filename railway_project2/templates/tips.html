Travel Tips

Smart tips for safe,comfortable and happy journey

1. Before You Travel
  • Book you ticket in advance
  • Check your PNR nad Status
  • Carry a Valid ID proof
  • Arrive at te station early
2. At the Railway Station
  • Check the platform number
  • Confirm the train and coach
  • Locate the coach position
  • Keep the ticket and ID easily accessible
3. Packing Tips
  • Keep your luggage within the permitted limit
  • Carry essential medicines and personal items
  • Label your luggage with contact details
  • Keep your valuables in hand luggage
4. Safety Tips
  • Dont leave valuables unattended
  • Avoid accepting food or drinks from strangers
  • Keep belongings secured while sleeping
  • Be careful when boarding and getting of the train
5. Overnight Journey
  • Carry a small travel pillow/blanket if needed
  • Keep your phone and vallet secured
  • Set an alarm before destination
  • Lock the luggage
6. Travel Responsibly
  • Dont litter the train/station,Use dust bins
  • Keep noise levels low
  • Respect fellow passengers
  • Dont pull the chain unnecessarily
