General Baggage Guidelines

Free Allowance

Passengers are allowed a free baggage allowance depending on the class of travel.

Excess Baggage

Excess baggage will be charged as per the railway rules and regulation

Prohibited Items

Carrying inflammable,hazardous or illegal items is strictly prohibited.

Left Luggage

Left Luggage facilities are available at major stations for your convenience.
